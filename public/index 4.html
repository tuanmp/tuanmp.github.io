Minh-Tuan Pham

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Irène Joliot-Curie Laboratory (IJCLab), Université Paris-Saclay, working on scientific machine learning for particle physics. My research spans graph neural networks for charged-particle track reconstruction in the ATLAS Inner Tracker, and generative models (normalizing flows, diffusion) for fast detector simulation. I completed my PhD at the University of Wisconsin–Madison and was previously a doctoral researcher at CERN.
