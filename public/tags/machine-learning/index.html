Hierarchical Graph Neural Networks for Particle Track Reconstruction

We introduce a novel variant of GNN for particle tracking called Hierarchical Graph Neural Network (HGNN), together with a learnable pooling algorithm (GMPool) and a dedicated loss function, showing improved tracking efficiency and robustness over traditional GNNs.

March 2023 · R. Liu, P. Calafiura, S. Farrell, X. Ju, D. T. Murnane, T. M. Pham
