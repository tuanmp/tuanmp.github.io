I am a postdoctoral researcher at the **Irène Joliot-Curie Laboratory (IJCLab)**, Université Paris-Saclay, where I work on scientific machine learning for high-energy physics. My research focuses on developing and deploying deep-learning methods for the ATLAS experiment at CERN.

---

#### Research interests

+ **Graph neural networks for tracking.** Reconstruction of charged-particle trajectories in the ATLAS Inner Tracker (ITk) using geometric deep learning, including heterogeneous and hierarchical graph models, metric-learning graph construction, and graph segmentation. I work on the GNN4ITk pipeline and its integration into the ATLAS software for HL-LHC reconstruction.
+ **Generative models for fast simulation.** Normalizing flows and diffusion models for fast, differentiable detector simulation, including improvements to the modelling of rare and extreme events.
+ **Scalable and efficient AI.** Training and inference optimization on GPUs and HPC systems---mixed precision, quantization, pruning, compilation---and benchmarking of AI models across hardware.

---

#### Background

+ **Doctoral researcher**, CERN (2022–2025).
